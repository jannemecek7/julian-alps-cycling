4. Requirement to provide data

Data marked as mandatory is needed to prepare an offer, make a booking or provide the service. If it is not provided, JAE may be unable to conclude or perform the contract. Consent to analytics, marketing or promotional images is not a condition for booking.

5. Recipients and processors

Authorised JAE personnel and contracted service providers may access data where necessary, for example accounting providers, hosting and email providers, analytics providers, advertising platforms, bicycle rental providers, guides or transport providers. Only data necessary for the specific purpose is shared, and partners are required to provide appropriate safeguards.

6. Transfers to third countries

Some providers, especially Google and Meta, may process data outside the European Economic Area. In such cases, mechanisms permitted by the GDPR are used, such as adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses and additional safeguards. The specific services in use are listed in the Cookie Policy and consent settings.

7. Retention periods

  • enquiries not resulting in a booking: normally up to 6 months after the communication ends;
  • bookings and contractual documentation: normally until expiry of limitation periods and longer where tax or accounting law requires;
  • invoices and tax records: for the periods prescribed by law;
  • data based on consent: until consent is withdrawn or the purpose ends;
  • records of consent and refusal: for as long as needed to demonstrate compliance;
  • safety incident or claim data: until final resolution and expiry of relevant periods.

8. Your rights

  • access to your personal data and a copy;
  • rectification of inaccurate data;
  • erasure where the conditions are met;
  • restriction of processing;
  • data portability for processing based on consent or contract by automated means;
  • objection to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • withdrawal of consent without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal;
  • a complaint to the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia.

Send a request to aljosa.dolhar@absbonifer.si. For security, JAE may require reasonable verification of identity. Requests are answered within the statutory period.

9. Automated decision-making

JAE does not make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects for individuals.

10. Data security

JAE applies appropriate organisational and technical measures, including access controls, secure passwords, system updates, backups and processor agreements. Absolute security cannot be guaranteed, so procedures are regularly adapted to risk.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated due to changes in services, technology or law. The current version and its effective date will be published on the website.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files or similar identifiers stored on or read from a user’s device by a website. They enable website operation, preference storage, usage measurement and, where the user consents, personalised advertising.

3. Consent management

On the first visit, the user is shown a consent manager offering equally prominent options: “Reject optional”, “Accept all” and “Settings”. Analytics and marketing cookies must not load before valid consent. The user can change the decision at any time through the “Cookie settings” link in the footer.

5. Browser settings

Users can also delete or block cookies in browser settings. Blocking necessary cookies may affect correct website operation. Browser settings do not replace the option to withdraw consent on the website.

6. Third parties and transfers

Google and Meta may process data for their own purposes in accordance with their policies. Because processing may take place outside the European Economic Area, JAE uses available contractual and technical safeguards and activates non-essential technologies only after consent.
